Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Land Motives LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information that you may provide via our website or offline interactions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked with you, including: personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, postal address), property information (Assessor's Parcel Number, state, county), and communication records when you contact us.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information directly from you when you submit a form to request a cash offer, subscribe to our communications, or interact with our team via phone or email. We may also collect public property data from local county and state records.

4. Use of Your Information

We use the information to: present cash offers for your property, coordinate real estate transactions with title companies, communicate with you regarding your property or our services, and improve our internal operations and marketing efforts.

5. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose your information to title companies, escrow agents, legal counsel, and service providers solely for the purpose of completing a real estate transaction or running our core business operations.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and understand where our audience comes from. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

7. Security of Your Information

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, or alteration. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

8. Policy for Children

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it promptly.

9. Opt-Out & Communication Preferences

You have the right to opt-out of receiving promotional communications from us. You can opt-out by following the unsubscribe instructions included in our emails or by contacting us directly to be removed from our call lists.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it regularly.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at info@landmotivesllc.site or call us at (307) 430-1099.
