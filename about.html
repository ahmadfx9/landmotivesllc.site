Our Story

Built on Wyoming Values. Driven by Real Solutions.

Land Motives LLC was founded in Sheridan, Wyoming with a simple goal: make it easy for property owners to sell their land, homes, and commercial properties without the headaches of traditional real estate. We saw an industry full of hidden fees, months-long timelines, and broken promises — and we decided to do it differently.

Today, we operate across four primary markets — Texas, Wyoming, Florida, and California — buying directly from motivated sellers with fast cash offers, zero agent fees, and a transparent closing process that takes an average of just 14 days. We're not a faceless corporation. We're real people who answer the phone, shake hands, and keep our word.

Our Mission

Fast, Fair & Transparent. Every Single Deal.

Our mission is simple: provide landowners and property owners with a transparent, fast, and stress-free way to sell. We believe in straightforward cash offers, covering all closing costs, and communicating directly without the corporate red tape.

We work with all property types — land, lots, single-family homes, multi-family homes, and commercial properties. We also actively partner with other wholesale companies through JV (Joint Venture) agreements, creating deal flow opportunities for everyone involved.

Core Values

What We Stand For.

These are the principles that guide every acquisition, every conversation, and every closing.

Integrity First

We say what we mean and we do what we say. Every offer we make is real, every timeline we give is honest, and every promise we make is kept. No bait-and-switch, no hidden clauses, no last-minute surprises.

Speed & Efficiency

We know your time is valuable. That's why we deliver offers within 48 hours, sign digitally, and close in an average of 14 days. We've stripped away every unnecessary step so you get paid faster.

Full Transparency

You'll never wonder what's happening with your deal. We keep you informed at every step — from initial offer to final payout. Open communication is not a perk for us, it's a requirement.

People Over Profit

We don't see parcels and plots — we see people with real situations. Whether you're dealing with inheritance, taxes, relocation, or just want to move on, we meet you where you are with empathy and respect.

Partnership Mindset

We don't just buy properties — we build relationships. From JV partnerships with wholesale companies to long-term connections with sellers, we're in this for the long haul. Repeat business is our best metric.

Community Growth

Every deal we close contributes to community development. By acquiring underutilized properties and connecting them with buyers who will put them to use, we help revitalize neighborhoods and create local value.
