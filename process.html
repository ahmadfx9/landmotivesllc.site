Our 4-Step Acquisition Process

A fully transparent, data-driven approach to buying your land without the traditional real estate headaches.

01

Submit Property Info

It all starts with a simple conversation or online form. Share your parcel number (APN), county, and state. We don't need professional photos or a survey. Just the basic details to let our acquisitions team begin their initial due diligence on zoning, topography, and market comps.

  • No commitments required
  • Completely confidential
02

Get Your Cash Offer

Within 48 hours, we analyze comparable sales data, assess county records, and evaluate development potential to present you with a fair, written cash offer. Unlike traditional buyers, our offer is firm and not contingent on bank financing approvals.

  • Data-driven valuation
  • Firm cash offer (no financing fall-throughs)
03

Sign & Open Escrow

Once you accept the offer, we send a straightforward 2-page purchase agreement. You can sign digitally from your phone or computer. The moment it's signed, we immediately forward it to a licensed, third-party title company to open escrow and begin the title search.

  • Simple, easy-to-read agreement
  • Trusted local title partners
04

Close & Get Paid

The title company prepares the final closing documents (deed transfer). A mobile notary can be sent to your location if you are out of state. We wire 100% of the funds to the title company, and they disburse your cash directly to your bank account or via cashier's check.

  • Zero closing costs for you
  • Mobile notary convenience

01
THE SCENARIO
Back Taxes Owed
WHAT HAPPENS
If you have years of unpaid property taxes, the county will eventually seize and auction your land at a tax deed sale. You lose the property entirely and get nothing.
HOW WE HELP
We work with the title company to pay off outstanding taxes from the sale proceeds, putting the remaining cash in your pocket before the county takes action.
02
THE SCENARIO
Inheritance & Probate
WHAT HAPPENS
Inheriting land with multiple heirs means coordinating signatures from siblings scattered across different states, often requiring costly probate court proceedings.
HOW WE HELP
We have experience buying inherited property, working through probate processes, and ensuring all parties are bought out fairly and legally through our title partners.
03
THE SCENARIO
Remote & Landlocked
WHAT HAPPENS
Properties without legal road access or in extremely remote locations scare away traditional buyers. These parcels sit unsold for years on the MLS.
HOW WE HELP
We specialize in raw land. We evaluate every property individually and often purchase parcels that traditional buyers wouldn't touch, including landlocked and off-grid land.
